# Utilities

`OMFrontend.ListUtil` and `OMFrontend.ArrayUtil` hold the list and array
functions the frontend is written with. They were the packages ListUtil.jl and
ArrayUtil.jl until OMFrontend 2.1.0 and keep their names and functions.
